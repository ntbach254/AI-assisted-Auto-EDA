**total_sulfur_dioxide** – Increases with higher free_sulfur_dioxide.  

**pH** – Decreases as fixed_acidity or citric_acid increase.  

**citric_acid** – Increases with higher fixed_acidity and decreases with higher volatile_acidity.  

**density** – Increases with higher fixed_acidity and higher residual_sugar.  

**quality** – Improves with higher alcohol and higher sulphates, but declines with higher volatile_acidity.  

**alcohol** – Increases as density decreases.  

**sulphates** – Decrease when volatile_acidity is higher.  

**fixed_acidity** – Rises with higher density and higher citric_acid, and falls with higher pH.  

**volatile_acidity** – Rises with higher citric_acid and falls with higher sulphates and higher quality.
