**Hours per week**  
Higher education levels are linked to more hours worked per week, and the average weekly hours differ across occupation categories.

**Income**  
Higher education (education_num) is associated with greater income, and more hours worked per week also relates positively to income. Age shows an association with income, and marital status predicts income, with the pattern varying by sex. Occupation categories explain additional income differences beyond education level. Overall, greater education, longer work hours, older age, marital status, sex, and occupation all relate to income variations.
